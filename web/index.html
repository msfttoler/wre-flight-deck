Start here: make and verify a fictional booking

Quick path: search SEA to DEN, check the fare, book with a unique fictional team name, and verify the reservation. Open the guide for exact clicks and expected results.

Open the exact 5 booking steps

The labels match this screen. Use fictional team names only; this local app does not issue real tickets or take payments.

  1. Find flights. Set From to SEA · Seattle, To to DEN · Denver, and Passengers to 1 passenger. Click Find flights. Wait for route options. If none appear, try fewer passengers or another destination.
  2. Choose an itinerary. Compare stops, UTC times, available seats, and base fare. Click Review fare on your choice. The fare form appears in Your fare, clearly.
  3. Check the full price. Set Checked bags to 1 bag / passenger. Enter FIRSTFLIGHT in Coupon (optional), then click Calculate fare. Read the price lines and Total · USD. The coupon discounts base fare, not bag fees. The quote expires after 15 minutes and does not reserve a seat.
  4. Confirm the booking. Enter a unique synthetic label, such as TEAM-ORBIT-A1, in Fictional team callsign. Click Confirm fictional booking once. Wait for BOOKING CONFIRMED and note the reference. Do not enter a person's name or payment details.
  5. Verify what was saved. Click View reservations in the confirmation, or choose Reservations in the workspace menu. Find your callsign; check its reference, route, total, and confirmed status.
Optional: update a departure and check its event
  1. In your reservation, note a flight number. Choose Departures. Find the same flight number and route, then click Edit. Only change a flight whose current status is Scheduled or Delayed; leave a departed flight alone.
  2. Set Departure status to Boarding. Set Gate to A12, then click Save departure. Check that the row shows Boarding and A12. If the save reports a conflict, refresh the schedule and review the current values before trying again. This change persists in this local workspace.
  3. Choose Event Journal. Find the flight.updated event for that flight and inspect its request ID. This is evidence from the local app only, not a GitHub webhook or CI run.
Optional: cancel only the booking you just made
  1. Open Reservations and find the unique callsign you entered.
  2. Click Cancel reservation. Check the callsign in the confirmation prompt. Click Keep booking if you are unsure; otherwise click Confirm cancellation.
  3. Check that the booking status is cancelled. Its seats are released. A booking cannot be cancelled after any flight leg has departed.
