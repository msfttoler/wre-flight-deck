# Flight Deck product context

## Register

product

## Users and scene

A cloud solution architect uses a laptop at a daytime workshop, then projects
the application in a dim meeting room. A visible light/dark choice must support
both settings. They need to understand a customer-facing engineering decision,
not learn an invented cockpit control language.

## Purpose and personality

A fictional regional-airline operations desk makes a connected application
concrete: route search, fare quote, reservation, departure and event evidence.
Precise, welcoming and practical. Flight vocabulary gives the app character
without obscuring ordinary tasks. Prefer "Find flights" to "Initiate vector".

## Principles

One obvious next action. Show the data and the reason a decision changed.
Use real API responses, not decorative metrics or simulated live telemetry.
Use progressive forms, readable route rows and an accessible geographic
overview. No prerequisite chains between sessions.

## Anti-references

No neon cockpit, glass cards, fake radar sweeps, endless card grids, gradients,
fabricated live feeds, padded success claims or animation for decoration.
